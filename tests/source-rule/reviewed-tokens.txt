-- Notes on the ones that looked suspicious:
--   meta, public, private: modifiers; a `meta def` is a declaration (gate rules 3 and 7).

--   from_lrat, lrat_proof: proofs checked by the kernel (axiom audit on the result).
--   unlock_limits, guard_min_heartbeats, unset_option: resource options (the harness caps apply).
--   assert!, debug_assert!, dbg_trace, panic!, unreachable!, println!: run-time, not elaboration.

--   init_quot, init_grind_norm: core initialisation commands (an error outside core).
