## Design choices

1. **The protocol is the toy, copied.** DESIGN §7.3 says `P_triv` is the toy protocol over the new
   relation. A submission must stand alone, so `Controls/Toy.lean` is copied and adapted, with
   credit in the header, and not imported. The only change of substance is the extractor's fuel
   split (item 3).
2. **`cfg = ⟨blake2s, 2⟩`.**
   - The hash menu has one entry.
   - Every oracle of `P_triv` has one leaf, so `Cfg.depth = 0` at every arity (`triv_depth`): no
     paths, and the index root is one leaf hash. The arity changes neither the tape nor the
     verifier's work.
   - 2 is the design's default. For a multi-leaf variant, arity 2 gives the shortest tape
     (`logs/S7/tape-size.log`: 89.2 / 92.2 / 96.1 / 104.6 MiB at arity 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 with leaves
     of 2^10 elements).
3. **Fuel split `![8, 0]`, not the library's `firstMsgImage` (= `atZero`, all of `B(k)` on call
   0).**
   - Call 0 (`extractMid 0`) is the only call a law constrains, and the image halts after its
     8 instructions. `run_firstMsgImage` shows that fuel `f + 8` suffices.
     `trivExtractor_fuel_tight` shows that 7 does not, on a small tape (`decide +kernel`).
   - The output call is unconstrained, because `trivKSF` at the last state ignores the witness.
   - Total: 8 against `B(24) = 2^64`.
   - What the kernel checks (corrected in the fix pass, S7-m3):
     - 8 steps suffice on every tape the protocol produces (`triv_extractMid`);
     - starving the call before round 0 breaks `Secure` (`split0_not_secure`, added in the fix
       pass).
4. **The certified class is 24**, the recursion tree's `k_min`: `L_chain(2^10)` is admissible at
   24 and not at 23 (re-measured, `logs/S7/lchain1024-sizes.log`). Board L needs the class of its
   largest leaf layout, 26 for the runner's default `L_chain(2^12)` (S7-M2, corrected in the fix
   pass).
5. **The challenge binds the extractor image** (S4-A2). This was first a conjunct of the stub.
   After the fix pass (S7-A1 → S7-A5) it is the field `Certificate.img` against `Declared.img`,
   rendered from the submitted `.bin`/`.data` files.

## The tape-size arithmetic (`logs/S7/tape-size.log`, `lchain1024-sizes.log`, `chain-report-1024.log`)

**The compiled `P_triv`.** Its shapes are static, so its tape is the same for every instance:

- the rounds section, which is the plain message, `8 · 2^(k+10)` bytes;
- the opening of the index oracle's one leaf, `8 · 2^(k+8)` bytes, with a path of depth 0. In
  "leaves × (leaf + path)" form that is `1 × (2^(k+11) + 0)`.

The query of the plain message opens nothing. The total is `5 · 2^(k+11)` bytes
(`triv_uncapped_length`). At `k = 24` that is **171,798,691,840 B (160 GiB)**:
**163,840× the 1 MiB cap** and **1,310,720× the 128 KiB cap**.

**The instance's content.** `L_chain(2^10)` at `k = 24` has:

- a witness of 25 heights + 10,232,905 cells, i.e. 81,863,440 B (78.1 MiB);
- a layout of 792,851 elements, i.e. 6.0 MiB.

That is 0.05 % of the compiled tape; the rest is padding. **The witness alone is 78× the 1 MiB cap
and 625× the 128 KiB cap**, so no protocol that sends the witness fits, whatever its shapes. The
best adaptive Merkle variant (open only the occupied leaves; minimised over leaf size and arity) is
84.5 MiB.
